## 1. Purpose

This GRC Program Charter formally establishes the Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Program of NimbusHR Technologies, Inc. The GRC Program provides a structured, enterprise-wide framework for identifying, assessing, monitoring, and mitigating cybersecurity risks that could adversely affect the Company's operations, clients, employees, financial condition, and regulatory standing. This Charter defines the authority, structure, objectives, and operating cadence of the GRC Program and is the foundational governance document for all related policies, standards, procedures, and controls.

## 2. Scope

The GRC Program applies to all NimbusHR business units, systems, data, personnel, contractors, and third-party vendors that access, process, transmit, or store Company or client data — in all geographic locations where NimbusHR operates or has regulatory obligations (United States and European Union). Scope encompasses all information assets hosted in AWS, accessed via Okta, managed through GitHub, and integrated via third-party APIs including but not limited to ADP, Stripe, Twilio, Snowflake, and PagerDuty.

## 3. Authority

The GRC Program is established by authority of the Board of Directors and operates under the executive sponsorship of the CISO, who holds ultimate accountability and reports GRC status to the Audit Committee semi-annually. The VP of Compliance and Risk holds day-to-day program ownership — resource allocation, program execution, and regulatory representation. The **GRC Analyst** serves as the operational lead for risk assessments, control testing, audit coordination, and compliance monitoring.

## 4. Governance Structure

```
Board of Directors / Audit Committee → CISO → VP, Compliance and Risk → GRC Analyst → IT Manager / Engineering Lead / Legal Counsel
```

## 5. Program Objectives

1. **Risk Identification and Reduction** — maintain a comprehensive enterprise risk register; ensure all material risks are identified, assessed, and assigned an accountable owner with an approved treatment plan.
2. **Regulatory Compliance** — ensure ongoing compliance with SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA, and NIST CSF, and maintain audit-ready evidence.
3. **Control Effectiveness** — design, implement, and test internal controls commensurate with the Company's risk appetite; track control gaps and drive remediation.
4. **Third-Party Risk Governance** — assess, monitor, and manage risk introduced by critical vendors through a tiered vendor risk management program.
5. **Security Culture and Awareness** — promote security awareness across all personnel through training, communication, and executive visibility into GRC performance.

## 6. Annual Review Cadence

This Charter is reviewed and reaffirmed annually, no later than January 31st, or immediately following any material cybersecurity incident, significant regulatory change, merger, acquisition, or material change to the Company's technology environment. Amendments require CISO approval and VP of Compliance and Risk acknowledgment.

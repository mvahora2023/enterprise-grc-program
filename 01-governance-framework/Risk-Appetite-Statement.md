# Risk Appetite Statement

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Effective Date | January 1, 2026 |
| Approved By | Board of Directors / Audit Committee |
| Review Frequency | Annually, or immediately following a material cybersecurity incident |

## Statement of Overall Risk Appetite

NimbusHR Technologies is committed to maintaining the trust of its 120 enterprise clients, protecting the personal data of over 1.2 million individuals, and sustaining its regulatory standing across applicable jurisdictions.

| Risk Domain | Appetite | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance / Regulatory | **Low** | Zero appetite for material violations of GDPR, CCPA, or SOC 2 requirements — treated as top priority regardless of likelihood |
| Cybersecurity | **Low-Moderate** | Some residual risk is unavoidable; all High/Critical risks require an active, documented treatment plan — none may remain unaddressed |
| Operational | **Moderate** | Moderate risk accepted for technology change, innovation, and scaling, provided compensating controls exist |
| Reputational | **Low** | Reputational damage from a breach or compliance failure is treated as existential given client dependency on SOC 2 certification |

## Risk Tolerance Thresholds (5×5 Matrix, Score 1–25)

- **Inherent score ≥ 20** → immediate escalation to CISO + VP Compliance; remediation plan approved within **30 days**
- **No risk with inherent score > 20** may go without an active mitigation plan for more than **90 days**
- **Residual score ≥ 12** → requires **quarterly** CISO progress reviews
- Formally risk-accepted items reviewed **at least annually** with CISO sign-off

## Domain-Specific Appetite

- **Data Privacy** — very low appetite. Any confirmed/suspected EU personal data breach triggers GDPR notification within **72 hours**; DSRs fulfilled within regulatory timelines without exception.
- **Third-Party Risk** — low-moderate appetite. All Tier 1 (Critical) vendors undergo annual assessments with documented findings; vendors failing minimum thresholds are escalated for renegotiation or replacement.
- **Availability** — moderate appetite, provided client SLAs are met. **RTO ≤ 4 hours, RPO ≤ 1 hour** for Tier 1 systems; unplanned downtime beyond SLA triggers root cause analysis.
- **Insider Threat** — low appetite given the volume of sensitive employee data processed. Privileged users are subject to background screening, access recertification, and activity monitoring.
